Bangladesh Visa on Arrival 2026: Rules & Requirements for Tourists

Some nationalities can get a tourist visa on arrival at Dhaka airport — typically for a stay of up to 30 days, with a fee payable on arrival — if they show proof of onward travel, a hotel booking and sufficient funds. It is discretionary and never guaranteed, so many travellers apply in advance instead. Check your route below.

Last verified 12 Aug 2026. Visa rules change often — always confirm with an official Bangladesh mission before you book flights.

⚠ Verify before you fly. Whether visa on arrival is available to you depends on your exact nationality and the policy in force when you travel, and it is granted at an officer's discretion. Confirm on the official Bangladesh e-visa portal at visa.gov.bd or with a Bangladesh embassy/high commission first. This page is guidance, not immigration advice.

What's usually required

  • A passport valid for at least six months, with blank pages.
  • Proof of onward or return travel.
  • A confirmed hotel booking or address for your stay.
  • Evidence of sufficient funds for your trip.
  • The visa fee, payable on arrival in an accepted currency.

Requirements vary and change, so treat this as a checklist to research — not a promise.

When to apply in advance instead

If your nationality isn't clearly eligible, if you want certainty, or if you're arriving overland or on a one-way ticket, arrange your visa ahead of time through the official e-visa portal (where available) or a Bangladesh mission. It removes the airport gamble entirely.

Common questions

Can I get a visa on arrival?

Some nationalities may, for tourism, subject to conditions and at an officer's discretion. It's never guaranteed. Confirm with an official mission before you fly.

What documents do I need?

Typically a valid passport, proof of onward travel, a hotel booking and evidence of funds — plus the fee. Exact rules vary by nationality.

How much is the fee and how long can I stay?

Usually up to 30 days, with a fee set by policy and payable on arrival. Verify the current amount on the official portal before travelling.

Is it guaranteed if I'm eligible?

No. If you want certainty, arrange a visa in advance through an official portal or embassy.
