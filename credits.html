Data & estimates

Cost ranges, day templates and visa groupings are compiled and maintained by hand for this project, drawing on public travel information and first-hand experience. They are indicative and updated periodically. Visa summaries are guidance only — the authoritative source is always an official Bangladesh government mission or e-visa portal.
