2-Week Bangladesh Itinerary & Trip Cost

Fourteen days is the sweet spot: enough to loop the whole country — Dhaka, the tea hills, the Sundarbans, the coast and the Hill Tracts — without living on night buses. A comfort-style two weeks costs about $1,450–3,300 per person before international flights. Open the loop in the planner to adjust it to your budget.

The loop

  • Days 1–2 — Dhaka. Settle in, Old Dhaka, and the rivers.
  • Days 3–5 — Sylhet & Srimangal. The tea hills, Lawachara and Ratargul.
  • Days 6–8 — Sundarbans. A mangrove cruise out of Khulna.
  • Days 9–11 — Cox's Bazar & the coast. The long beach, the southern road, and Saint Martin's if it's open.
  • Days 12–14 — Chittagong Hill Tracts. Kaptai Lake and the Bandarban ridgelines, then back out.

Two of these regions — Saint Martin's Island and the Hill Tracts — have access rules that change. Confirm both before locking your dates. See the Hill Tracts and coast pages for the current caveats, and the safety guide for the latest advisory.

Common questions

How much does two weeks cost?

A comfort-style fortnight covering most of the country typically runs $1,450–3,300 per person, excluding flights. Backpackers spend substantially less.

Can I really see all of Bangladesh in two weeks?

You can do a genuinely full loop. You'll still move a fair amount, so a buffer day or two saves the trip when a boat or road runs late.
