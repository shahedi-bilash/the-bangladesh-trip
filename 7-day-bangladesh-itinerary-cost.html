The Perfect 7-Day Bangladesh Itinerary + Cost (2026)

This 7-day Bangladesh itinerary is built for first-time visitors: Dhaka (2 days), the Sundarbans (3 days) and the tea hills of Srimangal (2 days), for a realistic comfort-style cost of about $750–1,700 per person before flights. The week, day by day

  • Day 1 — Dhaka. Land, settle, and ease in with an evening walk and street food.
  • Day 2 — Dhaka. Old Dhaka on foot, a Buriganga boat, and the old city by rickshaw.
  • Day 3 — to the Sundarbans. Travel to Khulna and board your cruise as the light drops.
  • Day 4 — Sundarbans. Dawn creek safari, watchtower walk, slow cruising between channels.
  • Day 5 — Sundarbans & back. A last forest morning, then the return toward Khulna.
  • Day 6 — the tea hills. On to Srimangal; walk the estates at golden hour.
  • Day 7 — Srimangal. Lawachara rainforest, Ratargul swamp forest, and the famous seven-layer tea before you fly out.

Common questions

Is 7 days enough for Bangladesh?

Enough for a strong first taste — the capital plus one or two contrasting regions. Going deep on two areas beats rushing four, because the long legs eat time.

How much does a week cost?

A comfort-style week like this often lands around $750–1,700 per person, excluding flights. Backpackers spend far less. The Sundarbans cruise is the biggest line.

When should I go?

Roughly November to February — cool, dry, and the classic window for the Sundarbans and hills.
