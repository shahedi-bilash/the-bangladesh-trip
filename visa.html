Common questions

Is "visa on arrival" guaranteed if I'm eligible?

No. Even where visa on arrival exists for your nationality, it's granted at the discretion of immigration officers and usually needs supporting documents — onward travel, a hotel booking, and proof of funds. Have them ready, and have a backup plan.

How long can I stay as a tourist?

Tourist stays are typically granted for a limited number of days and can vary by visa type and nationality. Check the exact duration on your visa or with the issuing mission.

Do I need proof of onward travel?

Very often, yes. Immigration and airlines may ask to see a departing ticket. It's wise to have one regardless of your visa route.

Where is the official e-visa portal?

Use only the official government portal or an accredited Bangladesh mission. We deliberately don't link a "portal" here because unofficial look-alike sites exist — search for the current official source or ask your nearest Bangladesh mission.
