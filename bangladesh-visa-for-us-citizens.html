Bangladesh Visa for US Citizens (2026)

Yes, US citizens need a visa for Bangladesh. Americans are generally eligible for a tourist visa on arrival at Dhaka airport (typically up to 30 days), if they show onward travel, a hotel booking and funds — or can apply for an e-visa in advance for certainty. It's discretionary, so verify the current rules before you fly.

Last verified 12 Aug 2026. Visa policy changes — confirm on the official portal before booking flights.

⚠ Verify officially. Confirm current eligibility, fee and stay length on the official Bangladesh e-visa portal at visa.gov.bd, or check the US Embassy in Dhaka. This page is guidance, not immigration advice.

The two routes for Americans

  • Visa on arrival (VoA). Available to US passport holders for tourism at Dhaka's Hazrat Shahjalal airport, subject to conditions and at an officer's discretion. Fastest if you qualify — but never guaranteed.
  • e-Visa / embassy in advance. Apply before you fly through the official portal or a Bangladesh mission. Best if you want certainty, are arriving overland, or hold a one-way ticket.

What US citizens need

  • A US passport valid at least six months, with blank pages.
  • Proof of onward or return travel.
  • A confirmed hotel booking or host address.
  • Evidence of sufficient funds.
  • The visa fee, payable on arrival in an accepted currency.

How long can you stay?

A tourist visa is typically issued for up to 30 days, sometimes extendable in-country. The exact length and fee are set by policy and change — confirm the current figures on the official portal.
