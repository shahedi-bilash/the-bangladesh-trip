A comfort-style trip to Bangladesh costs roughly $55–120 per person per day before international flights — about $400–840 for a week on the ground. What a 7-day trip costs

A comfort week combining Dhaka, the Sundarbans and the tea hills typically lands around $750–1,700 per person, excluding international flights — the mangrove cruise being the biggest single line. See the full worked breakdown on the 7-day itinerary & cost page, or open it live in the planner.

What a 2-week trip costs

Two weeks looping the whole country runs roughly $1,450–3,300 per person at comfort level. The 2-week Bangladesh budget breaks it down region by region.

Accommodation

Hostels and guesthouses run about $8–20 a night; solid mid-range hotels $30–70; the best rooms $90–220. Rooms in Dhaka and Cox's Bazar cost more than in Srimangal or the Hill Tracts.

Food

Street food and local restaurants are remarkably cheap — often $4–9 a day if you eat local, $10–22 for a mix of restaurants and cafés, and the highlight of many trips.

Transport

Local rickshaws, buses and trains cost very little. Intercity legs — the overnight bus or train to Khulna or Sylhet — are cheap; the cost adds up only if you take several domestic flights instead.

eSIM (mobile data)

A tourist eSIM data pack is about $6–18 for a typical trip and gets you online the moment you land — no SIM queue at the airport. See the first-timer guide for the full arrival checklist.

Travel insurance

Budget roughly $8–25 per week of travel. It's the one small cost worth never skipping, especially for the Sundarbans and the Hill Tracts.

Visa cost

Many nationalities pay a modest visa-on-arrival or e-visa fee. Check your route on the visa on arrival page (or the US citizens guide) — and always verify the current fee with an official mission before you fly.

Total: what to actually budget

Add it up and a realistic on-the-ground total is about $55–120 per day at comfort level, plus one-offs: the Sundarbans cruise ($180–450), any domestic flights, and your visa fee — before international flights to Bangladesh. The calculator totals it all for your exact regions, days and style.

Common questions

Is Bangladesh cheap to travel in?

Yes, by most standards. Backpackers travel on $20–40 a day; comfort travellers still find hotels and meals inexpensive by international norms. The Sundarbans cruise and domestic flights are the exceptions.

How much money do I need per day?

Roughly $20–40 backpacker, $55–120 comfort, $150–320 premium — covering hotel, food and local transport. The calculator gives your currency.

What's the single biggest cost?

The Sundarbans cruise, because its price bundles the boat, guide, permits and forest fees. See the Sundarbans cost breakdown.
